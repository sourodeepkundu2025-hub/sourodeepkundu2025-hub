## A timing decision that cannot wait six months

The current [Berkeley EECS admissions page](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/graduate/research-programs/admissions/) lists the Fall 2027 application deadline as **1 December 2026, 8:59 pm PST**. That is much earlier than this programme's April 2027 endpoint. If targeting that intake, prepare the application in parallel now; a later 2027 paper cannot be retroactively included in a December 2026 application. Recheck each live programme deadline before acting.

The [official admissions FAQ](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/academics/graduate/faq-3/) emphasizes letters from people able to describe research ability and potential. A GitHub star target is not a substitute for demonstrated contribution, research fit or detailed recommendations.

## Evidence package, not mass outreach

1. Select a specific research question and two recent papers from each prospective advisor/group.
2. Write a one-page fit note: what was reproduced, what failed, the mechanism being tested, why this group is relevant.
3. Maintain a two-page project brief with exact contribution, strongest baseline, raw-result link and limitations.
4. Ask existing research mentors early for feedback and, where appropriate, detailed letters based on work they know.
5. Prepare transcripts, language requirements, CV and programme-specific statements from each official checklist. Do not infer eligibility or funding from a laboratory topic match.
6. Contact a group only after reviewing its stated applicant-contact policy; no unsolicited messages or applications are sent by this plan.
